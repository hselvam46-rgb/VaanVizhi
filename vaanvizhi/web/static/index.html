Spatial Reconciliation & Mass Conservation Theory

Bridging the gap between 9 km coarse NWP and 1.0 km² panchayat realities

1. Additive Reconciliation (Temperature & RH)

$$\bar{T}_{block} = \frac{1}{\sum A_i} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A_i \cdot T_i$$ Ensures zero energy drift across the block domain while preserving microclimatic lapse-rate cooling along the Western Ghats slopes.

2. Expected-Value Precipitation Scaling

$$\mathbb{E}[R_{block}] = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (p_i \cdot \text{Amount}_i)$$ Solves convective rainfall clustering without violating regional hydrological forecasts.
